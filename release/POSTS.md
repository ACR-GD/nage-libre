## Before posting: things worth knowing

- **AI disclosure matters.** Opinions on AI translations are split right now.
  In June 2026 Romhack.ing stopped normal downloads of machine-translated
  patches after a backlash. romhacking.net's policy accepts machine-assisted
  translations "so long as a reasonable amount of effort has been put into
  editing". That's what the line-by-line proofread covers, so say so plainly,
  as these texts do. Expect a few negative replies anyway, and don't argue.
  Point people to the proofread and the issue tracker.
- **Never share the ROM** or a pre-patched ROM, and never link to ROM sites.
  Most forums ban it, and romhacking.net rejects archives that contain one.
- **Screenshots** for romhacking.net must be the raw 256×224 PNGs, with no
  upscaling or filters. The ones in `screenshots/` are exactly that.
- **Trailer:** the landscape trailer suits YouTube or a Reddit video post. The
  vertical one is for TikTok and Reels. Both show Varie's footage and music,
  which is normal for fan-translation promotion.
